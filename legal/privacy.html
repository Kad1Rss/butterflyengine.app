Privacy Policy

Butterfly Story Factory — Local Application · 2026

Butterfly Story Factory is a local application. It does not operate a server, does not have accounts, and does not collect or transmit telemetry.

What stays on your computer

  • Your story ideas, scripts, images, narration, subtitles, videos and reports are written to your local disk only. Outputs are saved under %USERPROFILE%\Videos\Butterfly Story Factory.
  • Your settings and job history are stored under %LocalAppData%\Butterfly Story Factory\data.

API credentials

  • Your Gemini API key is encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), scoped to your Windows user account, and stored only on your computer.
  • The key is sent only to Google's Gemini endpoints when you generate a story or narration, and is never stored or transmitted by Butterfly itself.

Third-party services you connect

When you use the application it calls the providers you configured (for example Google Gemini for text and narration, and Pollinations for images). Those providers receive the data needed to fulfil each request (the story prompt and narration text). Their own privacy policies apply to that data. Butterfly does not send data to any provider that you have not enabled.

This website

This marketing site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set marketing cookies or run third-party analytics. The demo video and images are served from this domain. If you email us, your message is handled by your email provider and ours.

Data retention and deletion

  • Production outputs are kept until you delete them.
  • The encrypted API key can be deleted at any time from the “API connection” panel. Uninstalling the application also removes the stored settings.

Contact
