Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need a Gemini API key?
Yes. The story and narration use Gemini, and you supply your own key. Butterfly encrypts it locally with Windows DPAPI. You pay Google directly for your usage.
Are my videos or key uploaded to Butterfly servers?
No. Butterfly has no cloud service. Outputs stay on your disk. Only the prompts and narration text needed for a request are sent to the providers you enabled.
What does it cost to run?
Images, music and subtitle timing use free or local providers. Your only variable cost is your Gemini API usage, billed by Google under your own account.
Can I use the videos commercially?
Yes. The Developer Edition license lets you monetize the videos you create. You remain responsible for the factual accuracy, rights and platform policies of your content.
Do I need Python or FFmpeg?
No with the installer — both the runtime and an LGPL FFmpeg build are included. The source ZIP is for buyers who prefer to install Python dependencies themselves.
Why is the installer unsigned?
Code-signing certificates are costly for an independent release. The build uses a one-folder layout without packing or code obfuscation. Windows SmartScreen may show an “unknown publisher” warning; choose “More info → Run anyway”.
